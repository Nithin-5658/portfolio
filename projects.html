02 MCA MINI PROJECT
ACADEMIC MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Timetable Generation and Management System

Automated Academic Timetable Management

A web-based system designed to generate and manage academic timetables for BCA, IMCA and MCA programmes, with dedicated access for Admin/HOD, Faculty and Students.

View Project Details

The system uses a constraint-based timetable generation approach to create schedules while considering academic and resource requirements.

  • Automatic timetable generation using recursive backtracking, MRV and candidate scoring.
  • Avoids class, faculty and laboratory allocation conflicts.
  • Supports consecutive periods for practical and project sessions.
  • Faculty can report unavailability for a specific date and selected periods or an entire day.
  • Supports date-specific daily rescheduling while keeping the published weekly timetable unchanged.
  • Timetable publication and notifications for Admin/HOD, Faculty and Students.

03 BCA MAIN PROJECT
STUDENT MENTORING PLATFORM

Mentor Link

Connecting Students, Mentors and Parents

A web-based mentoring platform designed for educational institutions to organize mentoring sessions, monitor student progress and improve communication between administrators, mentors, students and parents.

View Project Details

Mentor Link provides role-based access, allowing each user to access the features relevant to their responsibilities.

  • Admin: Registers students and faculty, assigns mentors, creates departments and batches, and oversees the system.
  • Mentors: Schedule mentoring sessions, update session details, send notifications and generate reports.
  • Students: View mentoring schedules, receive notifications and track their progress.
  • Parents: View mentoring reports and receive updates about their child's academic progress.
  • Provides secure role-based access and confidential user information.
  • Integrates real-time updates, automated notifications and reporting to improve communication and student support.

HTML CSS JavaScript Angular Node.js MySQL

04 BCA MINI PROJECT
ONLINE PAINT PRODUCT PLATFORM

Book My Paint

Online Paint Shopping and Ordering

A web application developed to make purchasing paint products easier and more convenient, allowing customers to explore products and place orders online.

View Project Details

The platform connects three stakeholders — Admin, Shop Owner and Customer — through a structured online paint-shopping system.

  • Admin: Manages categories, subcategories, brands and colours, verifies shops and oversees the platform.
  • Shop Owner: Registers a shop, adds available paint products and manages customer orders.
  • Customer: Logs in, searches paints by categories, subcategories, brands and colours, and places orders online.
  • Displays product details such as price, reviews, colours and product overview.
  • Customers provide booking dates and personal details, and can share product reviews and shopping feedback.
  • Supports online payments and separate user accounts secured by unique IDs and passwords.
